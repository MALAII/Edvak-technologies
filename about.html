Company Overview

Technical & Infrastructure Solutions Across Five Core Sectors

Edvak Technologies Pvt Ltd provides dependable engineering, contracting, and technical services tailored to commercial, institutional, and infrastructure requirements.

Our operations are structured into five dedicated business divisions: Electronics, Telecom, Electrical, PVC & Plumbing, and IT Services. Each division operates with qualified technical personnel, verified engineering standards, and robust field execution.

Rather than generic solutions, we work closely with facility owners, project managers, and organizations to ensure seamless installation, maintenance, and ongoing technical support across all our verticals.
